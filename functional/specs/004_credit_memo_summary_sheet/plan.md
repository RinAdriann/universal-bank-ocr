# Implementation Plan — Spec 004: Executive Credit Memo Summary Sheet

## 1. Architecture & Downstream Integration
- **Post-NER Data Pipeline Consumption:**
  - Consume `result["accounts"]` and `result["rows"]` generated by `extract_statement()` in `component/extractor.py`.
  - At this stage, all rows have already been processed through `layout.py` and `ner.py`, possessing finalized `counterparty_name`, normalized `date`, audit-checked `balance` / `printed_balance`, and valid `debit` / `credit` amounts.
  - Do NOT re-run OCR, layout parsing, or NER inference.
- **Module Separation (`component/memo.py`):**
  - Encapsulate all analytical aggregation, table layout, styling, and OpenPyXL writing in `component/memo.py`.
  - Maintain a clean boundary between raw document extraction and financial report generation.
  - **Zero Subjective Business Decisions:** The memo service acts purely as an objective aggregator and calculator. It does not attempt heuristic PINBUK or debtor-specific Bouwheer classification, leaving qualitative credit evaluation to the analyst.

---

## 2. Analytical Calculations & Business Logic (`component/memo.py`)
- **Account Metadata Extraction:**
  - `get_account_holder_name(acc_data)`: Inspects rows and account metadata to retrieve the account holder name (e.g., CV Selagan Jaya Transport, Herman Intoni).
- **Monthly Metrics Aggregation (`calculate_monthly_metrics`):**
  - Group transactions by `(year, month)`.
  - Calculate total `Mutasi Debet (MD)` and `Mutasi Kredit (MK)` per month.
  - Compute `Saldo Mengendap` per month: Average of daily closing balances utilizing `component.export.saldo_mengendap(rows)`.
  - Identify `Saldo Akhir (SA)`: Closing balance on the last active transaction day of each month.
  - Track transaction volume counts.
- **Standard Categorization & Top Counterparties Breakdown:**
  - **By Category (`component/transactioncat.py`):**
    - Group debits and credits by their standardized category (`Bank Fee`, `Interest`, `Payment/Purchase`, `Transfer In`, `Transfer Out`, `Salary`, `Cash Deposit`, `Cash Withdrawal`).
    - Present monthly volume and nominal totals per category.
  - **By Top Counterparty (NER-detected):**
    - Rank top $N$ ($N \approx 5\text{--}8$) counterparties for inflows (kredit) and outflows (debet) based strictly on `counterparty_name` extracted by the NER model, grouping the remainder under `Lain-lain`.

## 3. Excel Worksheet Layout & Styling (`create_sheet`)
Worksheet title: `00_RINGKASAN` (inserted at sheet index 0).

### Section 1: Portfolio Multi-Account Overview
- Columns:
  1. `No` (Centered)
  2. `Bank` (Left)
  3. `No Rekening` (Left)
  4. `Nama Pemilik` (Left)
  5. `Rata-Rata MD` (Right, Currency format `#,##0.00`)
  6. `Rata-Rata MK` (Right, Currency format `#,##0.00`)
  7. `Selisih (MK - MD)` (Right, Currency format `#,##0.00`)
  8. `Rata-rata Pengendapan` (Right, Currency format `#,##0.00`)
  9. `Saldo Akhir Terakhir` (Right, Currency format `#,##0.00`)
- **Total Row:** Formatted with top thin black border and double bottom accounting line, calculating totals via dynamic formulas: `=SUM(E{start}:E{end})`.

### Section 2: Per-Account Monthly Breakdown Matrix
- For each account:
  - Subheader with bank, account number, and account holder.
  - Table Columns: `Keterangan`, `<Month 1>`, `<Month 2>`, `<Month N>`, `Total`, `Rata-rata`, `MK - MD`.
  - Rows:
    - `MD`: Monthly debits, `=SUM()`, `=AVERAGE()`, and `=MK_avg - MD_avg` formula in the difference column.
    - `MK`: Monthly credits, `=SUM()`, `=AVERAGE()`.
    - `Pengendapan`: Monthly average balances, `=SUM()`, `=AVERAGE()`, and label `SA <Last Month>` in difference column.
    - `Saldo Akhir (SA)`: Last balance per month, `-` for total, `=AVERAGE()`.

### Section 3: Ringkasan Pihak Terkait (Counterparty Breakdown)
- Table Columns: `Pihak Terkait (Counterparty)`, `<Month 1> ... <Month N>`, `Total`, `% Share`.
- Rows aggregated by NER-extracted `counterparty_name` (with unnamed transactions grouped under `Lain - lain`).
- Formula `=SUM()` for row totals and column totals, plus `% Share`.

### Section 4: Rekapan Transaksi MK (Per-Account Credit Counterparty Recap)
- For each bank account:
  - Merged top header row 1: `<Bank>, <No Rekening>`
  - Merged top header row 2: `<Nama Pemilik>`
  - Gray column header row: `""`, `<Month 1> ... <Month N>`, `Total Result`, `Keterangan`
  - Counterparty rows for incoming (`Kredit`) transactions in that bank account, with `=SUM()` in `Total Result` and blank `Keterangan` column.
  - `Total Result` row with `=SUM()` across each month and total column.
  - Highlighted yellow bottom row merging the month + `Total Result` columns with `=AVERAGE()` across the monthly `Total Result` cells.

### Section 5: Rekapan Transaksi MD (Per-Account Debit Counterparty Recap)
- Same per-account table layout as Section 4 for outgoing (`Debet`) transactions in each bank account.

## 4. Pipeline Integration (`component/export.py` & `app.py`)
- **`component/export.py`:**
  - In `excel_bytes(result)`, invoke `create_sheet(writer.book, result, title="00_RINGKASAN")` to guarantee the memo sheet is positioned as index 0.
- **`app.py`:**
  - Add an executive memo preview expander (`st.expander("Executive Credit Memo Summary")`) displaying:
    - Summary metrics dataframe (Portfolio Overview).
    - Monthly turnover summary and Net Cash Flow (MK - MD).
    - Category distribution.

---

## 5. Non-Functional & Verification Constraints
- **Performance Budget:** Execution time must remain under $50\text{ ms}$ for 1,000 transactions across 5 accounts.
- **Robustness:** Seamless handling of single-account uploads, multi-account uploads, single-month statements, and missing counterparty names without throwing unhandled exceptions.
- **Data Integrity:** Verification that `=SUM` and `=AVERAGE` formula coordinates exactly match data row boundaries.
